Write a Python function `tax(income)`.

`income` is an int (annual income in rupees, >= 0). Compute progressive
income tax over three brackets:

- 0% on the part up to 100000,
- 10% on the part above 100000 up to 500000,
- 20% on the part above 500000.

Return the tax as an int, rounding down to a whole rupee.

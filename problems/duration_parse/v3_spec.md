Update: the ordering rule is gone — the client's inputs are generated by
humans and arrive like `"30m 1h 30m"`. Units may now repeat any number of
times and appear in ANY order; every term just adds its seconds to the
total (`"30m 1h 30m"` is 7200, `"1d 1d"` is 172800). Units, multi-digit
values, and flexible whitespace are unchanged.

Update: riders hate being assigned a car that's driving away from them. A
car moving AWAY from the call -- direction "up" with position above `floor`,
or "down" with position below `floor` -- is no longer eligible. A car
exactly at `floor` is always eligible whatever its direction. Among eligible
cars the old rule stands: nearest, tie to lowest index. If NO car is
eligible (a lone car heading away, or every car heading away), fall back to
the old rule over all cars -- somebody has to take the call.

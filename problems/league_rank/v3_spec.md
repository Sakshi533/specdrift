Update from the league committee: goal difference must no longer be the first
thing separating teams level on points — head-to-head record is. New tiebreak
order, replacing the old one: when EXACTLY TWO teams are tied on points,
compare the points won in the matches between just those two teams (3/1/0 per
meeting, counting both legs if they met twice); whoever took more
head-to-head points ranks higher. If their head-to-head points are level —
including when they never met — fall back to the previous chain (goal
difference desc, then goals scored desc, then fewer games played, then name
asc). Groups of THREE OR MORE teams tied on points skip head-to-head entirely
and use the previous chain unchanged.

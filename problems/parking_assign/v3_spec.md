Update: exact-size-first is dead -- an audit caught us turning cars away
from a half-empty lot. New placement rule for EVERY vehicle: take the
smallest spot size that fits it and has a free spot, first index within
that size. A car fits its own size and anything larger, and the old
one-size-larger cap is GONE: an S car may take an L spot when nothing
smaller is free. Motorcycles (`"XS"`) fit S, M, and L exactly as they
already do. No fitting free spot still means -1.
